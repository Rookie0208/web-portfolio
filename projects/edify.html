01 / Overview

Making learning resources easier to find.

Edify is an educational website designed to help school students access study materials from one central place. Instead of searching across multiple platforms, students can explore resources organised around their grade level.

The platform brings together notes, e-books and video tutorials in a straightforward interface, helping learners spend less time searching and more time studying.
