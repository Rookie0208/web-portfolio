01 / Overview

More than sneakers. A shared culture.

SNKR-Heads is a beta-stage website concept for an exclusive social platform centred on sneaker culture. Its goal is to bring people who share an interest in sneakers and streetwear into a dedicated online space.

The project explores the idea of building a community around a shared passion, with a distinct identity that speaks to sneaker enthusiasts rather than treating the experience as a generic social website.
