Problem & Thesis

Why group trips needed a dedicated digital home.

Group trips generate countless pieces of critical coordination data: itineraries, hotel bookings, who paid for fuel, who owes for dinner, and shared photos. In practice, this data ends up fragmented across Splitwise, WhatsApp chats, Google Photos, Apple Notes, and messy email inboxes.

This lack of a shared context causes duplicate work, awkward financial friction, and lost memories. Travel Buddy centers the entire experience around a single product object: the Trip.

⚡ The Fragmentation Problem

  • Tool clutter: Expenses on Splitwise, endless chats on WhatsApp, tickets buried in emails.
  • Floating-point calculation bugs: Inaccurate divisions that leave missing pennies and unbalanced totals.
  • Onboarding barrier: Traditional platforms force all travelers to create accounts before anything can be logged.
  • Post-trip debt friction: Confusing bilateral debts instead of simplified group repayments.

✓ What Travel Buddy Solves

  • Single context: Trip hub uniting members, expenses, balances, and history feeds.
  • Paise integer precision: All money computed in 64-bit atomic paise, enforcing a strict invariant zero net sum.
  • Claimable guest profiles: Add members immediately as guests; they can claim their profile upon sign-up with history intact.
  • Automated Excel exports: Multi-sheet audit reports (Summary, Settlements, Ledger, History) with 1 click.

Production Capabilities

Existing & Active Features

Currently implemented, tested, and functioning in the live application.

Identity & Secure Auth

Email registration with 6-digit OTP codes, JWT sessions, automated refresh token rotation, and password-confirmed deletion.

Trip Hub & Guest Claiming

Create trips, generate custom invite links (tripsplit://join/CODE), manage owner approvals, and let members claim guest profiles.

Paise-Precision Split Engine

Tracks expenses across Food, Stay, Transport, and Activities. Handles Equal and Custom splits without fractional rounding errors.

Debt Settlement Optimizer

Real-time net balance calculations (net = paid - share + repaid - received) and minimal transaction solver to clear debts.

Multi-Sheet Excel Reports

Server-side generation of native .xlsx files containing Executive Summary, Settlement Matrix, Itemized Ledger, and History.

Audit Feed & Push Alerts

Immutable timeline recording all trip events, contextual comment queries on expenses, and FCM notifications for mobile sync.
