The Experience

Technology that keeps travel moving.

Via.com is a travel technology platform that connects travellers, agents, airlines, and other travel service providers.

As part of the development team, I contribute to the backend systems that support flight searches, bookings, airline services, and related operations.

My work involves building new features, connecting external services, improving existing workflows, and resolving issues that affect the booking experience.

What I work on

  • Flight booking and post-booking services
  • Airline and travel partner integrations
  • Fare management and booking improvements
  • Backend automation and reporting
  • Production support and system improvements
Areas of Contribution

Different parts of the travel experience.

My contributions span multiple areas of the flight booking platform, from finding the right flight to managing services after a booking is made.

Flight Booking & Post-Booking

Worked on improving booking workflows and adding services such as seats, meals, and baggage. Also contributed to features that help travellers manage their bookings after ticket confirmation.

Airline & Supplier Integrations

Contributed to connecting airlines and travel partners with the platform, helping expand available flight options and support different booking requirements.

Fare Management

Worked on special fare programs, pricing-related workflows, and improvements to how flight options and fare information are presented.

Automation & Reporting

Built and improved backend workflows that simplify operational tasks, support bulk updates, and make important booking and refund information easier to track.
